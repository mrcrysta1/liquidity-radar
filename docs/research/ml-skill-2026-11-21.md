# Model skill vs simple trend rules (2026-11-21)

**Findings (written after the run; numbers below are the script's output, `npm run ml-skill`).**

- Over 8 years of walk-forward (25,157 trades on 24 markets) the model loses
  −0.022R per trade after costs and funding (95% −0.047 … +0.004, profit
  factor 0.96). It never cleared zero in a sustained way: 6 of 9 years negative.
- It does **not** choose sides better than simple rules on the same bars:
  vs following BTC's EMA(50) −0.017R (range −0.064 … +0.030), vs BTC EMA(200)
  −0.029, vs a coin flip +0.014 (−0.012 … +0.040). None is distinguishable
  from zero: no measurable skill.
- When it goes against BTC's trend (55% of its trades) it loses −0.051R per
  trade (range entirely below zero); with BTC's trend +0.015 (not proven).
  Its longs lose −0.041R (range below zero).
- The last 30 days (+0.256R, 399 trades) are the crash, not skill: on the same
  bars it is only +0.076R better than following BTC (range −0.160 … +0.312).
- Simple trend rules have no edge with this bracket either (BTC EMA(200)
  +0.002R): the 1.5R bracket over 12 bars is close to a coin flip minus costs
  for any side rule tested. Always-long is clearly negative (−0.053R).
- Verdict: the model's good live record comes from the market's direction.
  Before any new model (ML2) is built, it needs a target and inputs that can
  beat "follow BTC" in this same test.


Plan step 2. 24 markets, 4h, test bars 2018-07-18 → 2026-11-19 (each market's first 2000 bars only train). Every rule trades the model's bracket — stop 1.5 ATR, target 2.25 ATR, exit after 12 bars — from the signal bar's close, costs taker 0.05% / maker 0.02% / slippage 0.02% (the bot's own), plus the real funding paid or received. One trade at a time per market. Ranges are cluster-robust (entry day / week / month, the widest).

## Limits

- The walk-forward model is the bot's model as the gate tests it: refitted every 250 bars on the 2,000 before. The live model also retrains hourly, adds its own paper trades and corrects its probabilities online; those parts cannot be replayed over years and are not in this test.
- 4h bars: a bar touching both stop and target counts as the stop (as in the labels).
- The trend rules were fixed before the run (EMA 50 and 200, no tuning). Trades on the same candle across markets are not independent; the ranges account for that by clustering.
