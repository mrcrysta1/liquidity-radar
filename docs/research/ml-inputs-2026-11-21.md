# The model's 18 inputs, out of sample (2026-11-21)

**Findings (written after the run; numbers below are the script's output, `npm run ml-inputs`).**

- **Together the inputs make the model worse than no inputs.** Out of sample
  the network's log loss (0.698) is higher than a constant "training win
  rate" (0.687): −0.011 (95% −0.013 … −0.009), better in only 31% of test
  blocks, and the same in every period and every BTC regime. The network
  learns noise from its 2,000 bars; its probabilities are less reliable than
  knowing nothing. (This matches step 2: no edge after costs.)
- **Three signals, not eighteen.** 24 pairs correlate at 0.7 or more
  (rsi ≈ dist_ema20 0.98, ret48 ≈ ema20_vs_50 0.90, bb_pctb ≈ range_pos_24
  0.90 …). In practice: short-term stretch (rsi, dist_ema20, bb_pctb,
  range_pos_24, ret12, macd), the longer trend (ret48, ema20_vs_50,
  dist_ema50), and volatility / volume.
- **The one steady piece of information is a reversal effect.** The trend
  inputs correlate *negatively* with a long's result and positively with a
  short's: ema20_vs_50 rank IC −0.11 long / +0.10 short, the same sign in 76%
  of 1,269 blocks; ret48 and dist_ema50 −0.08 (71–72%). After a strong
  two-week rise, the next two days of this bracket tend to go worse for
  longs. It is small and the network does not turn it into profit.
- **No use found:** hour of day (hour_sin, hour_cos), one-bar taker-buy
  share, atr_rank_200 (helped before 2023, about zero or negative since);
  taker_buy_share_4 weak. The volatility pair and the time-of-day pair are
  not proven as groups either.
- **Caution on the "useful" column:** with inputs this correlated, shuffling
  one alone makes impossible combinations (rsi far from dist_ema20), which
  adds loss even when the input adds no information. The group rows and the
  rank IC are the more reliable reading.
- **The crash is at the edge of what it learned:** in the last 30 days the
  trend inputs sit 0.8–1.0 standard deviations below their history.
- For ML2 (not started): fewer, unrelated inputs (one per family), market
  inputs it lacks (BTC, breadth, funding), a simpler or more regularised
  model, and the bar to clear is "better than a constant" in this test and
  "better than following BTC" in step 2.

Plan step 3. 24 markets, 4h, 1269 walk-forward test blocks (250 bars each, 2018-08 → 2026-11), 628000 labelled outcomes (both sides of every test bar). Each block is scored by the model the walk-forward trained on the 2000 bars before it (same folds and seeds as walkforward.ts); nothing from a test block is used to train or tune. Labels: does the bracket (stop 1.5 ATR, target 2.25 ATR, 12 bars) make money before costs — the model's own target.

## Limits

- Shuffling measures what the trained model uses; an input the model ignores but that holds information shows up only in the rank IC column.
- Log loss differences are small numbers by nature (the outcome is close to a coin flip); the ranges say whether they are real.
- The live model also retrains hourly on its own paper trades and corrects probabilities online; those parts are not replayed here.
