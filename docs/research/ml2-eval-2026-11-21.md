# ML2 research — evaluation (2026-11-21)

**Findings (written after the run; numbers below are the script's output, `npm run ml2-eval`).**

- ML2 did not pass: 5 of the 7 checks failed on the holdout (2025-01 → 2026-11).
- Not one of the three models (logistic, small MLP, GBDT) predicted better
  than the constant average win rate, **even in the 2019–2024 design
  period** (gains −0.0005 … −0.0015, none above zero). Adding BTC trend,
  breadth, relative strength and funding did not change step 3's result.
- In trading terms: design about +0.01R per trade for M1/M2 (not different
  from zero); holdout −0.061R (range −0.272 … +0.151), worse than following
  BTC EMA(200) (+0.019R) and not better than a coin flip.
- Reading: the problem is the question, not only the inputs. "Does a
  1.5 ATR stop / 2.25 ATR target bracket over 2 days win?" is close to a coin
  flip for every coin and side, and nothing in price, BTC, breadth or funding
  history predicts it. A different question (a longer horizon, or a filter
  on F1's trend trades as C6 does) would be a new proposal.
- As planned, ML2 stops here: no second round on the same holdout, nothing
  goes to paper trading.

## Limits

- Survivorship: the 24 coins are today's list. Funding before ~2019-09 is a default rate. Open interest is not available as free history.
- 4h candles: a candle touching both stop and target counts as the stop; entries at the next candle's open (as the labels).
- Three models on one target and one input set; the holdout was run once, for the chosen model only.
