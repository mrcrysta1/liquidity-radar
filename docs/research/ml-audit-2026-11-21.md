## 2. Trade states (as implemented)

- Real trades (`bot_trades.status`): `open`, `closed`, `error`.
  Exit reasons: `tp`, `sl`, `time`, `trail`, `signal`, `manual`, `error`.
- Closing in progress: `closing:<id>` = `time | tp | error | signal | trail`
  (persisted, retried every tick). This is the directive's CLOSING state.
- Paper trades (`bot_shadow_trades.status`): `open`, `closed`, `expired`.

Mapping to the directive's states:

| Directive | Existing | Gap |
|---|---|---|
| PENDING_ENTRY | none | Market order sent, DB row written right after. A crash in between leaves an unrecorded position; the next tick flattens it (safe, but the trade is lost from the record). Small gap. |
| OPEN | `open` | — |
| PARTIALLY_CLOSED | none | Not needed: no strategy exits in parts. Partial *entry* fills use `executedQty`. |
| TAKE_PROFIT / STOP_LOSS_TRIGGERED | exit_reason `tp` / `sl` / `trail` on close | Recorded at close, not as a live state. Enough for 4h. |
| CLOSING | `closing:<id>` | — |
| CLOSED | `closed` | — |
| EXECUTION_ERROR | `error` | — |

Only the PENDING_ENTRY marker is worth adding.

## 3. F1 exit logic (unchanged, by design)

`strategy.ts`: 3 ATR initial stop; on each 4h close the stop trails to the
4 ATR chandelier, never looser; exit on close ≤ EMA(300) or close < 10-bar
low. Real exits are the exchange stop (any time) or a market close after a
candle-close rule fires. Study #182: break-even, partial, tighter trail and
EMA(20) exits were all worse; EMA(50) +0.019R (not significant).

## 4. ML architecture (as implemented)

- **Models:** per coin, two MLPs (long and short) = 48 networks for 24 coins.
  18 inputs → 6 tanh neurons → sigmoid. Adam, L2 2e-3, 60 epochs, early stop
  on the last 20% of training rows (`mlp.ts`, `walkforward.ts` DEFAULT_WF).
- **Inputs (18), all from the coin's own 4h chart:** returns over 1/4/12/48
  bars in ATR, RSI, MACD histogram, distance to EMA20/EMA50, EMA20 vs EMA50,
  Bollinger %b, ATR %, ATR rank (200), volume vs 20-bar average, taker-buy
  share (1 and 4 bars), position in 24-bar range, hour of day (sin/cos).
- **Label:** does a trade with stop 1.5 ATR / target 2.25 ATR (BOT_SL_ATR,
  BOT_TP_ATR → 1.5R) / 12-bar time exit make money before costs. A bar that
  touches both counts as the stop.
- **Decision:** EV = p·(avg win − cost) − (1 − p)·(avg loss + cost) per side;
  best side if EV ≥ 0.1R (BOT_MIN_EV) and the setup rules pass.
- **Online correction:** p' = σ(a + b·logit p) per coin × side × regime
  (EMA20/50 trend × ATR tercile = 6 regimes → 288 correctors), one gradient
  step per closed real or paper trade (`learning.ts` updateCalibration).
- **Retraining:** every hour or 20 closed trades per coin a challenger is
  trained on ~2,000 bars **plus the model's own closed paper/real trades**;
  it replaces the champion if it beats it on the last 24 h (log loss and
  expectancy, ≥ 20 outcomes).
- **Gate:** walk-forward (2,000-bar train, 250-bar test, purged) must show
  ≥ 60 trades, ≥ +0.05R, PF ≥ 1.15 before the model may trade real money.

Live record since Nov 1: 2,744 challengers trained, 245 promoted (about 10
champion changes per coin in three weeks), 132 (5%) passed the walk-forward
gate. ML is paper-only (`ml:shadow`).

## 5. Problems found in the ML (with evidence)

1. **Direction, not skill.** Nov 1–21: 81% of paper trades short; shorts
   +0.72R, longs −0.45R; BTC −29% in the same weeks. Losing days are bounce
   days (Nov 2–3, 15–16). The long-history walk-forward fails the gate 95% of
   the time — the long test does not confirm the 3-week result.
2. **Blind to the market.** No BTC, breadth, cross-coin or long-trend
   (EMA200/300) input; no funding or open interest. Each coin is judged alone,
   so all coins flip together and 24 trades act as one bet (mean |net side|
   per candle 0.64).
3. **Training counts the same weeks twice.** Challengers train on the bars
   and on the model's own trades from the same bars
   (`learning.ts` splitForChallenger → `walkforward.ts` fitModel `extra`).
   Those trades are appended last, so early stopping validates on them: the
   most recent trend gets the most weight.
4. **Selection on a 24 h holdout.** About 6 bars per coin; with hourly
   challengers the champion changes on luck (~10 times per coin in 3 weeks)
   and follows the latest swing.
5. **Online correction learns the trend.** Predicted p rose 0.49 → 0.65 by
   week (actual 0.46 → 0.81). Correlated trades move 288 correctors in the
   same direction; when the trend flips they are confidently wrong.
6. **Regime label is too thin** (EMA20/50 × volatility). The 7-class regime
   in `src/context/market.ts` (BTC-based, PR #183) is not used by the model.
7. **No sideways detection.** Nothing tells the model a market is ranging;
   reversal trades earn +0.01R per candle.
8. **Tiny data per net.** 48 separate nets on ~2,000 rows each, while the
   coins share most of their behaviour; a pooled model has 24× the data.
9. **No portfolio view.** No cap on same-side ML trades per candle.

## 6. Backtesting and labels — assumptions

- 4h OHLC only. TP and SL in the same bar → stop (conservative), for labels,
  paper trades and the walk-forward. No 1-minute data used anywhere.
- A touched TP counts as filled at the TP price with the maker fee. Live, the
  TP is a post-only limit; a touch without trading through may not fill.
- ML costs: taker 0.05%, maker 0.02%, slippage 0.02%. Funding is not in ML
  labels or paper trades (it is in real-trade P&L). Research scripts (`study`,
  `audit`) use 0.10% per leg plus real funding.
- Paper trades enter at the signal bar's close; real trades at the next
  fill (slippage measured since #181).
- Labels and outcomes: the training label is the bracket outcome. Paper and
  real outcomes are stored separately (`bot_shadow_trades`, `bot_trades`), but
  both are fed back into training (`extra`). There is no table for
  counterfactual exit-policy results yet; the exit study keeps them in its
  report only.

## 7. Missing — intrabar research

- 1-minute kline loader + cache (Binance public, free).
- Intrabar resolver: which of TP / SL came first inside a 4h bar; still
  "ambiguous → stop" when both fall in the same minute.
- Fill model for the TP limit (require a trade-through, e.g. one tick past).
- A report of how many labels change (old vs new) and what that does to
  expectancy.

## 8. Missing — ML intelligence

- Shared market inputs: BTC return / EMA distance / volatility, breadth
  (% of coins above EMA50 and EMA300, mean 12-bar return), coin's own move
  vs BTC (residual), rolling correlation and beta, long trend (EMA200/300
  distance), funding rate.
- The 7-class regime and a sideways (range) flag as inputs or as an abstain
  rule.
- Clean training: no double-counted trades, purged and embargoed holdouts,
  per-candle weighting.
- Stable selection: longer pooled holdout (e.g. 2 weeks, all coins) and a
  significance test before a champion changes.
- Calibration pooled per side on a rolling window, slower, judged per candle.
- A skill benchmark: ML minus a "follow BTC's trend with every coin" rule on
  the same candles.
- Pooled / larger networks, only as separate experiments.
- Portfolio exposure: same-side cap and correlation-aware heat for ML.
