# ML2 research plan — design and pass criteria (2026-11-21)

Status: **proposal for the owner's approval. Nothing is implemented.**
Basis: steps 1–3 (PRs #190–#192): the current model has no measurable skill
2018–2026 (−0.022R/trade, no better than following BTC or a coin flip), its
probabilities are worse than a constant, and its 18 inputs are ~3 signals.

## Fixed rules

- The original model (`ml`, 62 networks, backup #1) is not retrained,
  overwritten or changed. It stays paper-only.
- F1, C2, C6, entries, exits, stops, targets and risk settings are not touched.
- ML2 is research only until the owner approves the next stage; then paper
  only (its own strategy id `ml2`, shadow). Never promoted automatically
  (PR #189 already guarantees this for strategies configured shadow).
- Every choice below is fixed **before** the first run, so the test cannot
  be tuned to the answer. Anything added later is reported as a new try.

## 1. What ML2 predicts

The same question as the current model, so the results compare directly with
steps 2–3: for each coin and 4h candle, does a long (or a short) with stop
1.5 ATR, target 2.25 ATR and a 12-candle time limit make money before costs?
One target only. A different target or horizon would be a separate,
later proposal.

## 2. Inputs (12, one per idea, all known at the candle's close)

| # | Input | Family | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Coin 48-candle return (in ATR) | coin trend | the one steady signal in step 3 |
| 2 | Coin distance from EMA(20) (in ATR) | coin stretch | stands in for RSI, %b, range position (r ≥ 0.88) |
| 3 | Coin ATR as % of price | coin volatility | |
| 4 | Coin volume vs 20-candle average (log) | coin volume | |
| 5 | BTC distance from EMA(200) (in ATR) | BTC trend | the baseline to beat uses it |
| 6 | BTC 48-candle return (in ATR) | BTC momentum | |
| 7 | BTC ATR as % of price | BTC volatility | crash / calm |
| 8 | Share of the coins above their EMA(50) | breadth | is the whole market moving |
| 9 | Average 12-candle return of the coins (in ATR) | breadth | the market's recent push |
| 10 | Coin's own move vs BTC, 48 candles (residual after beta) | relative strength | moving with or apart from BTC |
| 11 | Coin's 30-day correlation with BTC | relative | how much BTC drives it |
| 12 | Coin funding rate, 3-day average | positioning | crowded longs / shorts |

Dropped from the current 18: hour of day, one-candle taker share, ATR rank
(no use in step 3), and the duplicate momentum / stretch inputs.
Not available: open-interest history (Binance gives only the last 30 days
free), so it is left out.

Leakage guards (tested): every input uses candles up to and including the
decision candle only; breadth uses only coins that were trading at that time;
beta and correlation use the 30 days before; funding only settled rates.

## 3. Models (3 candidates + 1 baseline, all pooled across the 24 coins)

One model for all coins (24× the data of today's per-coin nets), long and
short sides separately, inputs scaled on the training data only.

| Id | Model | Why |
|---|---|---|
| M0 | Constant: the training win rate per side | the "knowing nothing" bar step 3 used |
| M1 | Logistic regression, L2-regularised | simplest; hard to overfit |
| M2 | Small neural net (12 → 6 → 1), stronger L2, early stopping | today's kind of model, done right |
| M3 | Gradient-boosted trees (the GBDT C6 already uses) | non-linear, robust to scale |

No tuning on the test data: settings are the existing defaults (M2: the
current MLP settings with L2 ×5; M3: C6's defaults).

## 4. How it is tested

- **Walk-forward**, as steps 2–3: train on the 2 years before each 250-candle
  test block, all coins pooled; 12 candles purged and 12 embargoed between
  train and test, so no label reaches across.
- **Two periods:**
  - Design: 2018-07 → 2024-12. Used to compare M1–M3 and choose **one**.
  - Holdout: 2025-01 → 2026-11. Run **once**, for the chosen model only.
    It is the evidence.
- **Trading test:** the bot's own rule. Trade the side with the higher
  expected value if it is ≥ 0.1R, one trade at a time per coin, the same
  bracket from the candle's close. Costs are the bot's (taker 0.05%, maker
  0.02%, slippage 0.02%) plus real funding, and again at a conservative
  0.10% per side.
- **Same-candle comparison** with the "follow BTC EMA(200)" rule and with a
  coin flip, as in step 2.
- **Statistics:** 95% ranges clustered by day / week / month (whichever is
  widest), because one BTC move drives many coins at once.
- **Reported:** log loss, Brier and calibration; R per trade, win rate,
  profit factor and drawdown; by year, by the 7 BTC regimes, longs vs
  shorts, and the last 30 days separately.

## 5. Pass criteria (all must hold on the holdout)

1. **Beats knowing nothing:** log loss better than M0, 95% range above zero.
2. **Beats following BTC:** on the same candles, R per trade better than the
   BTC EMA(200) rule, 95% range above zero.
3. **Makes money after costs:** mean R per trade above zero at the bot's
   costs, and not below zero at 0.10% per side.
4. **Not one lucky stretch:**
   - positive in bull, bear and sideways BTC regimes taken as three groups,
     or at least not clearly negative in any of them;
   - no single month brings more than 40% of the total R.
5. **Both sides behave:** neither longs nor shorts clearly negative (95%
   range entirely below zero).
6. **Calibrated:** its predicted win rates within 5 points of the real ones
   in each decile with ≥ 200 outcomes.

If it passes, it goes to stage C (below). If it fails, ML2 stops there, with
the report. No second round of tweaks on the same holdout.

## 7. Known limits

- **Survivorship:** the 24 coins are today's list; coins that died are not in
  the test, which flatters any long-biased rule.
- **Funding history:** before ~2019-09 it is missing and filled with a
  default rate.
- **Holdout size:** about 23 months. A small real edge may not show clearly
  there; a fail then means "not proven", not "proven useless".
- **Multiple tries:** 3 models on one target and one input set. Kept small on
  purpose; the holdout is used once.
- **4h candles:** a candle that touches both stop and target counts as the
  stop, as in the current labels. Intrabar data (plan step 4) is not part of
  this plan.
